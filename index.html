New in 1.1

Share with any phone, and three new Pro themes

Share with any phone

Send photos and videos to an iPhone or Android next to you, in original quality — no internet, no account, no compression.

Share by link

Create a 7-day link through your own Google Drive. Anyone can download the originals in Auramy or a browser.

Shared with You

Everything you receive lands in its own gallery category, and backs up to Drive like the rest.

Three Pro themes

Titanium, Emerald and Aurora restyle every screen, down to the icons.

Know your storage

One ring for what is used and what is left, then a breakdown by camera photos, videos, screenshots and downloads — with the real size of every file, which Photos never shows you.

  • Sort by size, date or type
  • Search with words like “selfie 2024”, “>100mb” or “last week”
  • On This Day memories on Home

Share with any phone, in full quality

Nearby sharing sends originals straight from one phone to another, iPhone or Android. On the same Wi-Fi it finds the other phone by itself; without one, scan a code and the two connect directly.

  • Original files — Live Photos and Motion Photos included
  • No internet and no account needed
  • Remove location before sending, if you like
  • Free, with no limit

No Wi-Fi? Scan and send

The receiving phone shows a code; the sending phone scans it and the transfer starts — at Wi-Fi speeds, with no network around. For someone far away, share a 7-day link from your own Google Drive instead.

  • Works between iPhone and Android
  • A link opens in Auramy, or in any browser
  • Stop sharing a link at any time

Clear the clutter in one tap

Deep Clean finds exact duplicates, near-identical bursts, blurry and accidental shots, and screenshots you are done with — one-time codes, used tickets — and keeps the best shot of each burst.

  • See everything before anything is deleted
  • Favourites are never touched
  • Deletions stay recoverable for 30 days
  • Exact duplicates are free to clear

A gallery that stays out of the way

Camera photos, videos, screenshots, downloads and Shared with You, each as a plain row you can scroll. Pinch any grid to zoom, just like Photos.

  • Photo and video editor built in
  • Back up a whole category in one tap
  • Private vault behind its own PIN

Six looks, one app

Light and Dark are free. Neon, Titanium, Emerald and Aurora come with Pro and restyle every screen, down to the icons.

And everything else

Back up to your own Google Drive

Back up and restore whenever you want, free. Auramy asks only for the “drive.file” permission, so it sees nothing in your Drive but the files it created.

Free up space safely

Remove from your phone what is already safe in Drive — only what has a verified copy.

Shrink videos, keep the memory

Re-encode 4K clips to 1080p with their date and location intact. A result that isn't meaningfully smaller is thrown away.

Restore to a new phone

Connect the same account and Auramy brings back what your backup has and the phone doesn't.

Widgets and shortcuts

Storage and photo widgets, Shortcuts and Control Centre on iPhone, Quick Settings tiles on Android.

Locked when you want it

An optional Face ID or fingerprint lock on the whole app.
